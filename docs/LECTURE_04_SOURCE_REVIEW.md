## Research, food claims and trade
- **Survey data:** Sary's team reported approximately 73% taste and 43% speed among preliminary responses. The raw instrument, population, sampling process and respondent count were not supplied here; these numbers are labelled *team-reported, non-generalizable* until independently reviewed. Follow AAPOR transparent sampling/questionnaire guidance: https://aapor.org/standards-and-ethics/best-practices/
- **Food safety:** Pure Bite's diabetes/allergen/lactose-related claims remain proposals. Names of ingredients or “natural” positioning alone do not establish safety or suitability. Allergen cross-contact and substantiation must be reviewed; legal labeling obligations differ by jurisdiction, product and sales format. FDA educational reference (US, **not Egyptian law**): https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-food-labeling-and-critical-foods/food-allergies
- **Export prices:** Hussein's orange oil project cited an *indicative historical/student-reported* FOB figure around USD 17,000/tonne; treat it as neither a verified current price nor a quote to the university. Verify product classification, HS code, reference period, volumes and trade terms before using it in a financial model.
- **Trade terms:** FOB and CIF differ in freight/insurance responsibilities. Official ICC Incoterms® overview: https://iccwbo.org/business-solutions/incoterms-rules/ ; rule detail: https://library.iccwbo.org/clp/clp-incoterms.htm?AGENT=ICC_EST
- **Brand anecdotes:** Volvo, VW, United 2017, Coca-Cola/Pepsi 2004, Kinect and S Pen are used as classroom illustrations; no unverified usage percentages, sales-growth rates, universal claims or spurious legal statistics are asserted.
