## Classroom-faithful examples and caveats

- Intermediary access to retail chains and the instructor's farm retail experience are presented as experiences illustrating negotiation barriers, not verifiable universal listing durations or requirements.
- Instant-noodle outlet-fit debate, B2B export route, selected distributors, technical generator advice and temperature-sensitive delivery are context examples. Participants' personal names and raw exchanges are intentionally not published.
- The class's distribution partnerships, courier comments and commercial margin percentages are not promoted into verified claims about named companies.
- Any resale-price limits or territorial arrangements may raise competition-law issues. The public lesson explains channel conflict and calls for local legal review, instead of prescribing fixed resale pricing.
- **Different hypothetical café scenarios:** Action-plan example: **500 to 600 monthly orders**; prepared KPI exercise: **100 to 120 monthly orders**, actual 110, ad cost 3,000, 60 attributed orders, 50 cost/order and ROAS 2.4×. The two baselines are deliberately identified as separate scenarios; no genuine market or business performance is asserted.
- **Attribution ≠ causation; ROAS ≠ profit; cost per attributed order ≠ CAC** without suitable data/definitions.
- Core project submission instructions remain authoritative in the already-approved Project Kit ZIP on the Project page. The lecture provides a study reminder; it does not create new grading or delivery policy.
