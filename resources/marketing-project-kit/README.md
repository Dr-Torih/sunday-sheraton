## Core academic requirements from the approved Sunday course

- Deliver one integrated **Marketing Decision Journey** across the 8 cumulative stages, not 8 standalone projects.
- Final Word report: **15–20 A4 body pages**, excluding cover, contents, references, appendices.
- Final student presentation: **10–12 slides**.
- Evidence per team: **at least 30 valid surveys, 10 direct interviews, 3 credible secondary sources**.
- Formal assessment (Sunday course): **70 marks** (written plan 50, presentation and defense 20).
- For the Sunday course, Lecture 05 is the last teaching session and Lecture 06 hosts presentation/defense; all project deliverables are due three full days before Lecture 06. Exact due dates for other cohorts must be provided separately.
